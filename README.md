GLOAMING ARCHIVE — a top-down stealth game in a candle-lit archive at night. You are a novice slipping through the stacks to recover the scattered pages of a banned book and reach the exit stair unseen. Lantern wardens patrol with vision cones that shelves and curtains block; standing in lantern light fills the great detection eye fast, deep shadow nearly freezes it, and sneaking low behind desks or into curtains and tall shadow-grass breaks sight entirely. Toss pebbles to pull wardens off their routes. Six chambers, three warden kinds (patrol wardens, sweeping sentries, and hounds that scent you even in shadow), light-and-shadow hiding, ghost/shadow/owl ratings, three difficulties, and an Endless Watch mode. Everything is original code, art and synthesized WebAudio sound in this repo; it plays fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 — or simply open game/index.html in a browser
```

## Play

- **WASD / arrows** — move · **Shift** — sneak (slow, silent, hidden in grass, low behind desks)
- **Click** a floor tile — toss a pebble lure; wardens investigate the sound
- **P** pause · **R** retry chamber · **M** mute · **Enter** confirm · **1/2/3** difficulty on the title screen
